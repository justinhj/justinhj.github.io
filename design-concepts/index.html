Functional[Justin]
reimagined ×5

Five completely different directions for the blog. Four homepages and one article page. Each one is a working prototype built on real post data, so click around, use the keyboard, and hover over things.

01 / homepage
:Neovim //buffer
The homepage is a Neovim session. Use j/k to move, / to open a Telescope finder, : for commands, and space for which-key.
 02 / homepage
Type Signature
A Swiss editorial programming journal. The brackets in the name become the frame of the page, and each post is written as a Scala signature.
 03 / homepage
The Category
A glowing graph in deep space. Tags are objects and posts are morphisms. Pick two tags to compose them and see the posts they share.
 04 / homepage
The Stream
The archive as a Kafka topic, styled like an industrial control room. Partitions, offsets and consumer-group filters.
 05 / article page
Literate
A post page that mixes Tufte, Knuth and Org-mode. Sidenotes, foldable star headings and src blocks, with light and dark themes.
Prototypes only. Nothing on the live site has changed.
